# Alicia Recorder {#english}

Alicia Recorder for macOS turns recordings into transcripts, detailed summaries, minutes and a daily journal, from
work and from private life: meetings, talks, lessons, calls, conversations, voice memos. Transcription runs on your
Mac.
{: .lead}

**Coming to the Mac App Store.** Free to try for 15 days, then a one-time purchase.
{: .note}

## Transcription on your Mac

- Transcription and speaker separation run on your Mac, with speech models included in the app.
- Parakeet transcribes 25 European languages, SenseVoice Mandarin and Cantonese, Whisper the other languages. A
  meeting that switches between French, English and Chinese is transcribed in all three.
- Save a speaker as a person once, and that voice is suggested as the same person in later recordings.
- Search finds words, and meaning across languages, with a search model that also runs on your Mac.

## Summaries with the AI model you choose

- Summaries by topic in the language you pick, each point citing the sentences it comes from; minutes in time order;
  a short daily journal and a weekly review.
- Ask questions about your recordings and get answers that cite their sources.
- These need a language model that you set up yourself: a cloud provider with your own API key, or a model server on
  your Mac or local network, such as Ollama or LM Studio. Without one, the app still transcribes, separates speakers,
  searches and exports.

## Your files, your provider

- Recordings, transcripts, summaries and journals are ordinary files in a folder you choose, for example in iCloud
  Drive.
- The app never sends audio to an AI provider, only text, and nothing goes to a provider outside your Mac and local
  network before you agree to it. Mark a recording “Local Only” and it stays with local models.
- No sign-up, no analytics, no server of ours. The [privacy policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}) lists what the
  app sends, when, and to whom.

Requires macOS 26 and a Mac with Apple silicon. The interface is in English and Simplified Chinese.

# Alicia Recorder 简介 {#chinese}

macOS 上的 Alicia Recorder 把录音变成逐字稿、细致的总结、按时间的纪要和每天的日记，工作和生活都适用：会议、讲座、课程、电话、聊天、语音备忘。转写在你的 Mac 上完成。
{: .lead}

**即将上架 Mac App Store。** 可以免费试用 15 天，之后一次性购买。
{: .note}

## 在你的 Mac 上转写

- 转写和说话人分离在这台 Mac 上完成，语音模型随 App 提供。
- Parakeet 识别 25 种欧洲语言，SenseVoice 识别普通话和粤语，其他语言由 Whisper 识别。法语、英语、中文来回切换的会议也能转写。
- 把说话人存成人物以后，之后的录音里同一个声音会被提议为这个人。
- 搜索既找字词，也按意思跨语言查找；搜索模型同样在这台 Mac 上运行。

## 用你选的大模型写总结

- 按话题的总结，用你选的语言写，每一条都引用原话；按时间顺序的纪要；精炼的每日日记和每周回顾。
- 对录音提问，回答注明出处。
- 这些需要一个你自己设置的大模型：填你自己的 API key 使用云端服务，或者使用本机、局域网里的模型服务，例如 Ollama、LM Studio。没有大模型时，转写、说话人分离、搜索和导出照常可用。

## 文件和服务都由你决定

- 录音、逐字稿、总结和日记都是普通文件，放在你选的文件夹里（例如 iCloud 云盘）。
- App 从不把音频发给 AI 服务，只发文字；在你同意之前，什么都不会发给本机和局域网以外的提供方。把一段录音标成“仅本地”，它就只交给本地模型。
- 不用注册，没有统计分析，我们不运营服务器。[隐私政策]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}#chinese)写明了 App 发送什么、什么时候发、发给谁。

需要 macOS 26 和搭载 Apple 芯片的 Mac。界面有简体中文和英文。
